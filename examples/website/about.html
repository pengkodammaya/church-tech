About Us

Ordinary people, extraordinary grace.

Our story

Grace Community Church began in 2015 when twelve neighbours started meeting in a living room to read the Bible and pray for their town. Today we are one family across two generations and three languages — still doing the same simple things: loving God, loving people, and serving our community.

What we believe

The Bible

God speaks to us through Scripture. It is our guide for faith and everyday life.

Jesus

Jesus is God’s Son, who lived, died, and rose again so that anyone can be restored to God.

Grace

We are accepted by God’s grace, not by earning it. So everyone has a seat at the table.

Our mission

  • Love God passionately
  • Love people genuinely
  • Serve our community practically
  • Share good news courageously
